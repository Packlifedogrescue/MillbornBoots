The Craft

From hide to heel

We take no shortcuts. Each pair takes several weeks at the bench.

  1. 01

    Select

    We choose each hide by hand and cut the panels to avoid flaws and follow the grain.

  2. 02

    Last

    The upper is pulled tight over a wooden last and left to take its shape.

  3. 03

    Welt

    A Goodyear welt is stitched in, so the sole can be replaced again and again.

  4. 04

    Finish

    We burnish the edges, dress the leather and inspect every stitch before the pair leaves the shop.

Care

Keep them going

How do I break in my boots?

Wear them for short stretches at home for the first week. The leather and cork footbed will mold to your foot. Expect them to feel snug at first; that's how they should feel.

How often should I condition the leather?

Every 1–3 months, depending on wear and weather. Brush off any dirt, work in a light coat of conditioner, let it absorb overnight, then buff.

Can you resole my boots?

Yes. Every Millborn boot is welted so it can be resoled. Get in touch and we'll arrange it.

How long does a custom order take?

Lead times vary with the season and the size of our queue. We'll give you an honest estimate when you inquire.
